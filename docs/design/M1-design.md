## Modelling notes

- Booster ascent Isp is the mean of SL and vacuum; M2 replaces it with the trajectory-integrated value.
- Fairing counted as upper-stage dry mass for its whole burn (conservative).
- Max-q loads use the design values in the spec; M2 checks the trajectory stays within them.
- Bending ignores inertial relief and pressure stabilisation (both conservative).
